What Skilled Bounties is

Skilled Bounties is a skill-bounty marketplace. People in India post short tasks, other people claim them, and payment is collected by Razorpay. Supabase provides sign-in and the database, intended to be hosted in Mumbai (ap-south-1).

Skilled Bounties’ in-app payment record is a ledger of those Razorpay charges. It is not a wallet, a stored-value facility, or a regulated escrow.

Personal data we process

Account data: name, email address, password (held by Supabase Auth as a hash), and the session.

Profile data: display name, handle, bio, skills, avatar colours, ratings, and verification status.

Task data: bounty text, chat messages, ratings, and reports you file or that name you.

Payment references: Razorpay order, payment, refund, and transfer identifiers, and amounts in Indian rupees (paise). Skilled Bounties does not store full card numbers, UPI PINs, or bank account numbers. Razorpay processes those as a payment aggregator.

Device data: a local flag that separates offline demo mode from a real session, a language preference, and a device-local list of people you have blocked.

Optional feature data: a referral code and who referred you; Crown Pass dates (when a subscription product is offered); Skill Gem progress derived from completed tasks and ratings; gift-bounty recipient names and codes; Live Room session times; identity-check status; an earnings-streak counter and non-cash sparkle balance; and, if you use Voice-to-Bounty, the text of a dictation sent to a drafting service. Counsel should review the lawful basis for each of these.

Why we process it

To create and secure your account, show your profile, run the bounty and chat, take and reconcile Razorpay payments, operate optional features (Rush Gem, Crown Pass, referrals, gifts, Live Room, voice drafts, price guidance, dispute window, and a verified-helper badge), investigate reports, and meet tax and accounting duties that apply to THE CROWN LEGACY.

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 requires a lawful purpose and notice. Creating an account is the point at which you give consent for this processing. You can withdraw consent by deleting the account or writing to the grievance officer, subject to records we must keep.

Who else sees it

Supabase processes account and database data as a processor for THE CROWN LEGACY. Razorpay processes payments as a payment aggregator. If a Live Room provider (for example Daily, Agora, or Jitsi) or an identity-check provider is later connected, or if a drafting LLM is called, that provider will process the data needed for that feature. Those providers have not been chosen yet. Other Skilled Bounties users see your public profile, badges, your messages inside a task you share, and ratings you leave.

We do not sell personal data. We disclose it when a law, court, or regulator in India requires it, or when a payment dispute needs the task record.

How long we keep it

Profile and chat data stay until you delete the account or we close it, unless a bounty, claim, or ledger row is still attached. The current database refuses to erase a person who has bounty or payment history, because those rows are restricted or append-only. In that case the app suspends the account, removes the public profile text, and the grievance officer must finish erasure where the law allows it.

Payment records may be kept for the period required by Indian tax and accounting rules that apply to THE CROWN LEGACY. The exact retention period is to be confirmed by counsel.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you may ask for a summary of personal data, correction, erasure, and nomination of another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to do so. You may withdraw consent. You may complain to the Data Protection Board of India after using the grievance process below.

In the app, open Profile and choose Delete account. That is the in-app erasure request Apple requires. If the server cannot erase the login yet, the screen says so and does not pretend the account is gone.

Children

Skilled Bounties is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly create accounts for children. If you believe a child has an account, write to the grievance officer and we will close it.

Security

Sign-in uses Supabase sessions. Payment secrets stay on the server. Row-level security limits which rows a signed-in person can read. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.
