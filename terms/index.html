The marketplace

Skilled Bounties lets a poster offer a short paid task and a helper claim it. Skilled Bounties is not the helper’s employer, not a law firm, and not a financial adviser. A task is a contract between the poster and the helper. Skilled Bounties provides the listing, the chat, and the Razorpay payment flow.

You must be at least 18 and able to contract under Indian law. One person, one account. You are responsible for the activity on your account.

Fees

A bounty must be priced between ₹5 and ₹1,000. The platform fee is 12% of the agreed bounty price, or 8% of that price while a poster has an active Crown Pass at the time of posting. The helper’s recorded share is the price minus that fee rate. Rush Gem is an optional add-on of ₹29, ₹39, or ₹49 for a 10-minute turnaround. The whole Rush Gem amount is platform revenue and is not shared with the helper. The poster is charged the agreed bounty price plus any Rush Gem, on the same Razorpay order, and that amount is held until the poster approves the work. Example without Crown Pass or rush: a ₹100 bounty records a ₹12 fee and ₹88 for the helper. Amounts are stored in paise.

Crown Pass is intended as a ₹199 per month subscription that also adds priority listing and a badge. Live Razorpay Subscriptions are not enabled in this build. Any in-app activation is a test-mode stub and does not start a real recurring charge. Counsel must review subscription cancellation, auto-renewal, and refund wording, and a chartered accountant must confirm GST, before Crown Pass is sold.

Razorpay’s own processing charges are not added as a second fee in the in-app breakdown. GST on Skilled Bounties’ fee, and any TDS or TCS that applies to an e-commerce operator, is to be confirmed by a chartered accountant. The 12% or 8% figure above is the product fee, not a tax invoice.

Payments, refunds, and disputes

The poster pays through Razorpay (UPI, card, netbanking, or another method Razorpay offers). A bounty opens only after Razorpay confirms the captured payment. The screen inside the app is a record of that payment. It is not a wallet and not a regulated escrow.

Before someone claims an open bounty, the poster can cancel. A captured payment is then refunded through Razorpay to the original method. After a claim and before release, either person can open a dispute. After release, Dispute Shield lets the poster open a dispute within 24 hours of payment. The payment record stays frozen until Skilled Bounties resolves it by releasing or upholding the helper’s share, or refunding the poster (including any Rush Gem) through the existing refund path. Counsel must review this 24-hour post-payment window, the standard for “work is not right,” and how it sits with the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

A Route transfer to a helper’s linked account happens only after the poster approves and only if Razorpay has activated Route for THE CROWN LEGACY and the helper’s linked account. Ordinary Razorpay access does not mean Route is on. If Route is not active, approval updates the record and does not send a bank payout.

Gift a Bounty lets one person pay for a task on behalf of a named friend, who receives a share code or link. The payer is charged. The legal relationship between payer, friend, and helper, including who may approve work or open a dispute, needs counsel review before this is offered outside a demo.

The poster and a helper may negotiate the price (accept the listed amount or send a counter-offer within ₹5–₹1,000, for a limited number of rounds). The agreed price locks before payment. A Razorpay order is created only for that agreed amount. The poster pays in advance. The amount is held in Skilled Bounties’ payment record and is released to the helper only after the poster approves the work, or refunded through the existing refund path if a dispute is resolved in the poster’s favour.

All payments must go through Skilled Bounties. You may not ask for or send payment off the platform, including UPI IDs, phone numbers used for payment apps, or phrases such as “pay me directly.” The app may mask or block that content. Off-platform payment to avoid the fee is prohibited. NEEDS COUNSEL: review this clause, the negotiation and pre-payment lock, and the chat-masking practice before publication.

Referral Crystals issue a ₹50 ledger credit to both the inviter and the friend after the friend’s first completed (paid) bounty, subject to anti-abuse limits (no self-referral, one code per person, a cap on grants per inviter). Credits are a promotional balance against future Skilled Bounties charges, not a wallet withdrawal and not cash. Tax treatment of promotional credits must be confirmed by a chartered accountant. Counsel should review prize, payment-system, and advertising rules before a public launch.

Offline demo mode uses play money. It is not a payment, a refund, or an account with THE CROWN LEGACY.

Not professional advice

Tasks in Legal, Money, or any other category are practical help between people. They are not legal advice, tax advice, medical advice, or a regulated professional opinion. Do not treat a bounty as a substitute for a licensed professional. Helpers must not present themselves as giving that advice.

Prohibited tasks and conduct

You may not post, claim, or discuss: anything illegal in India; sexual content involving anyone under 18; weapons, explosives, or violent wrongdoing; fraud, phishing, or stolen data; impersonation; harassment; off-platform payment arranged to avoid Skilled Bounties’ fee; or a request for regulated advice dressed up as a small task.

We may remove a task, refuse a payout, or suspend an account that breaks these rules. You can report a person or a task in the app. Reports are stored for review. Blocking someone hides them on your device; it does not yet remove them for every other user, because the database has a reports table and not a separate global block list.

Your content

You keep ownership of the text you post. You give Skilled Bounties a licence to host, display, and store it so the marketplace can run, including showing it to the other person in a task and to reviewers of a report or payment dispute.

Account deletion

You can ask to delete your account from Profile, without emailing support first. If you have no bounty or payment history, the server deletes the login. If bounty, claim, or ledger rows exist, the current database cannot drop those rows. The app then suspends the account, clears the public profile text, signs you out, and tells you the login and ledger remain until erasure is finished. Write to support@thecrownslegacy.in to complete that request.

Skill Gems, Live Room, and AI helpers

Skill Gems (Quartz, Amethyst, Sapphire, Ruby, Diamond) are reputation badges based on completed tasks and rating. Higher gems may suggest a higher price, but every bounty is capped at ₹1,000 for now. A gem is not a professional licence.

Live Room is an optional 15-minute in-app voice or video session with a timer. Until a provider key is configured, the room is a stub and does not carry a real call. When a provider is connected, that provider’s terms also apply. Do not use a Live Room for content that is illegal in India.

Voice-to-Bounty and the AI Price Guide may suggest a title, description, price, or time. Suggestions can be wrong. You are responsible for the bounty you post. If no drafting key is set, the app uses on-device heuristics and does not send the text to an LLM.
